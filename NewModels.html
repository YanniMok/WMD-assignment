Current Models

Here you will get to witness all the current models in all their greatness and glory. With the current models get to see the advanced technology, the luxurious advancements. Whether you are looking for raw power or comfort, it is all available throughout every model.

Huracán

The Huracán is designed for raw power and to turn heads of every spectator. Packed under the hood is a 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 engine giving you a whopping 572–640 horsepower. This beast goes 0–100 km/h in 2.9 seconds with a top speed of 320–325 km/h. It also features dynamic steering, magnetorheological suspension and an adaptive network intelligent management system.

From P4,300,000 – P8,300,000
Temerario

Redesigned with a new look while retaining the same powerful performance. The Temerario rocks a mid-mounted V8 engine with forced induction via twin turbos, revving up to 10,000 RPM — a first for a production engine. Being a hybrid, three electric motors contribute to a combined 907 horsepower.

Approx. P5,900,000
Urus

The Urus brings the SUV form factor to Lamborghini's DNA. A 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 engine pushes up to 641 horsepower, launching the SUV from 0–100 km/h in under 4 seconds. Also available in hybrid form, with electric motors driving the wheels for added efficiency without sacrificing performance.

From P3,300,000
Revuelto

The most prestigious current model in the Lamborghini lineup. A 6.5-litre V12 engine spins to 9,300 RPM, pushing a hefty 814 hp — combined with three electric motors (one per front wheel, one in the 8-speed dual-clutch transaxle) for 147 HP more, totalling 1,001 HP. Batteries charge via regenerative braking or plug-in.

P107,200,000
