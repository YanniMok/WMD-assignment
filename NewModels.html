Current Models

Here you will get to witness all the current models in all their greatness and glory. With the current models get to see the advanced technology and the luxurious advancements. Whether you are looking for raw power or comfort, it is all available throughout every model.

[ Huracán ]
V10 · Naturally Aspirated

Huracán

Designed for raw power and to turn heads of every spectator. Packed under the hood is a 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10 engine giving a whopping 572–640 horsepower. This beast goes 0–100 km/h in 2.9 seconds with a top speed of 320–325 km/h. Features dynamic steering, magnetorheological suspension, and an adaptive network intelligent management system.

From ₱4,300,000 – ₱8,300,000
[ Temerario ]
V8 Hybrid · Mid-Mounted

Temerario

Redesigned with a new look and the same powerful performance. Rocking a mid-mounted V8 engine with forced induction via twin turbos, revving up to 10,000 RPM — a first for a production engine. A hybrid, with three electric motors contributing to 907 combined horsepower.

Approximately ₱5,900,000

[ Urus ]
SUV · Twin Turbo V8

Urus

The SUV face of Lamborghini and a very popular option in the current market. Features a 4.0-litre twin turbo V8 pushing up to 641 horsepower. Goes 0–100 in under 4 seconds. Also available in a hybrid model allowing power to wheels through electricity.

From ₱3,300,000
[ Revuelto ]
V12 Hybrid · 1,001 HP

Revuelto

The most prestigious current model of the Lamborghini brand. Packs a 6.5-litre V12 engine with 9,300 RPM pushing 814 horsepower, combined with three electric motors — one per front wheel and one in the 8-speed dual-clutch transaxle — adding another 147 HP for a total of 1,001 HP.

₱107,200,000
